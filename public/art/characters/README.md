# Pokémon hero artwork

`charizard-home.png` is the 512 × 512 transparent Charizard HOME artwork distributed by [PokeAPI's sprites repository](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites).

Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokeAPI/sprites/master/sprites/pokemon/other/home/6.png

Retrieved September 9, 2026. The original PNG is unmodified. Pokémon character artwork belongs to its respective rights holders; this is not RIPDEX-generated artwork. Breathing, fire, embers and Poké Balls are separate CSS/SVG presentation layers in `apps/web/src/pokemon-hero.ts`.
